# 🌍 Global Supply Chain Intelligence & Analytics

### End-to-End Data Analytics for Global Trade, Economic Indicators, Logistics & Supply Chain Risk

> **A comprehensive Data Analytics project transforming global trade, economic and supply chain data into actionable business intelligence using Python, SQL and Power BI.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Global supply chains operate across multiple countries, industries and economic environments. Changes in trade activity, economic conditions, logistics performance and market demand can significantly influence business operations and supply chain decisions.

This project analyzes **global trade, economic indicators, logistics and supply chain performance** to identify patterns, relationships, operational trends and potential areas of supply chain risk.

The solution follows an end-to-end **Data Analytics and Business Intelligence workflow**, beginning with data preparation and transformation in Python, followed by structured SQL analysis and interactive Power BI reporting.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations operating across global markets need visibility into:

* Trade performance across countries and regions
* Economic conditions affecting supply chain activity
* Import and export trends
* Logistics and operational performance
* Country-level supply chain exposure
* Supply chain risk indicators
* Changes in demand and market activity
* Opportunities for improving supply chain efficiency

Without a consolidated analytical view, decision-makers may struggle to identify emerging risks, compare markets and understand the factors influencing global supply chain performance.

### This project addresses that challenge through data-driven analysis.
